Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026
Developer: VEXTRON GAMING
App: Math Quest: Math Adventure / Математический квест
Android package: vex.gaming.mathquest

This policy covers the Android game and messages you send to its support address. The game does not require an account. We do not operate a server to store your game progress.

1. Information used by the game

InformationHow it is used
Level completion, stars, language, sound and music preferencesStored on your device to continue your game and remember your settings.
Support correspondenceIf you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send so that we can respond. Do not send passwords, signing keys or payment details.
Advertising identifiersThe integrated Yandex Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share device identifiers when available for advertising. Yandex documents HTTPS/TLS protection for this transmission. Location tracking is disabled in the game.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The game displays banner and interstitial advertisements and offers optional rewarded advertisements for hints. Advertising is provided by Yandex Mobile Ads. Learn about its processing in the Yandex Privacy Policy and SDK data disclosure. Advertisers' pages opened from an ad are governed by their own policies.

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photographs, or precise location. We do not ask for your name or email address to play. We do not integrate a separate analytics or account service in this version.

3. Your choices

You can change language, sound and music in the game's settings. You can skip rewarded ads. Manage or delete your advertising ID in your Android privacy or advertising settings, where supported; the options depend on the device and Android version. These controls do not necessarily remove advertising from the game.

4. Retention and deletion

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until the app's data is cleared or removed. Android backup and restore settings may affect whether data is restored after reinstalling. Clearing data removes your saved progress.

Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle your request and any applicable obligations. To request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us, email vextrongaming.dev@gmail.com. We may need enough information to identify your request. For information held by Yandex, use its privacy resources or contact us for help identifying the appropriate request route. We cannot promise immediate deletion of data controlled by another provider.

5. Intended audience

The game is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

6. Security and international processing

We limit the information requested by the game and do not publish support messages. No storage or communication method is completely secure. Advertising, email and hosting providers may process information in countries other than your own under their respective policies.

7. This website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising, analytics scripts or embedded tracking services added by us. GitHub may process technical request information to operate the hosting service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Updates and contact

We update this page when relevant practices change and revise the effective date above. For privacy questions and requests, contact VEXTRON GAMING at vextrongaming.dev@gmail.com. Your rights depend on applicable law; you may also contact your local data protection authority.

2. Реклама и сторонние сервисы

В игре есть баннерная и межстраничная реклама, а также добровольный просмотр рекламы за подсказку. Рекламу предоставляет Yandex Mobile Ads. Язык, звуки и музыку можно изменить в настройках игры. Просмотр рекламы за подсказку можно пропустить. Эти настройки не обязательно отключают показ рекламы.

Локальные настройки и прогресс остаются на устройстве до очистки или удаления данных приложения. Резервное копирование Android может влиять на восстановление после переустановки. Очистка данных удаляет сохранённый прогресс.

Переписку с поддержкой мы храним столько, сколько необходимо для рассмотрения обращения и выполнения применимых обязательств. Для доступа, исправления или удаления отправленных нам сведений напишите на vextrongaming.dev@gmail.com. Может потребоваться информация для идентификации обращения. По данным, которыми управляет Яндекс, воспользуйтесь его ресурсами конфиденциальности либо обратитесь к нам за помощью в выборе способа подачи запроса. Мы не можем обещать немедленное удаление данных у другого поставщика.

Игра предназначена для взрослых от 18 лет и не ориентирована на детей. Если вы считаете, что ребёнок отправил персональные данные в нашу поддержку, свяжитесь с нами для проверки и удаления таких сведений при необходимости.
